# Privacy

Applies to RadioHub Basic at hallowelt42.github.io/RadioHub. RadioHub Basic runs entirely in your browser: no account, no cookies, no ads, no analysis of your behaviour.

## Responsible

HalloWelt42

## Opening the page

The page is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, USA). When you open it, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes. See the [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The legal basis is the legitimate interest in delivering the page securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Listening to radio

The station list is part of the page. Nothing is transmitted when you search or filter. When you play a station, your browser connects to it directly. The station then sees your IP address, details about your browser and that the request comes from this page. Many stations use further services, also outside the EU. The privacy policy of the respective station applies.

## Station logos

Your browser loads the logos in the list directly from the servers of the stations, as soon as the list is shown. The same details are transmitted.

## Podcasts

When you search for podcasts, your search term and your IP address go to the directories of Apple (iTunes Search) and fyyd. Your browser loads episode lists, images and audio directly from the servers of the podcasts you open or have subscribed to.

## What is stored in the browser

Appearance, language, volume, favourites, rules, search history, last station played, podcast subscriptions with listening position, alarms and, if you enter one, a location for sunrise and sunset. These details stay in the storage of your browser and are not transmitted to us. They are necessary for the functions you asked for (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG, Germany). You can delete them in your browser via the website data.

The page only asks for your location when you press the button for it and allow it in the browser.

## Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and you can complain to a data protection authority. We ourselves have not stored any data about you.
